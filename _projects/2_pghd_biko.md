**A Study on Establishing the Foundation for Linking and Collecting Patient-Generated Health Data (PGHD) in Republic of Korea National Bio Bigdata**
<br>Korea Health Information Service (KHIS) · Nov 2025 – Oct 2026

Over 11 months, we ran a pilot study with **187 participants** recruited through Samsung Medical Center, collecting consumer wearable (**Samsung Galaxy Watch**) and **continuous glucose monitoring (CGM)** data to evaluate the feasibility of integrating PGHD into **BioBigData.Korea (BIKO)**.

My role:

- Developed quality-control criteria for wearable and CGM data, assessed participant adherence and data completeness, and proposed core variables along with the minimum collection period and sample size for future large-scale collection
- Conducted an extensive literature review of PGHD collection in biobanks and population cohorts
