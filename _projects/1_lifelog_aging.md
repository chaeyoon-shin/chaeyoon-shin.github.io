Wearable lifelogs from Samsung Galaxy Watch and from Fitbit devices in the *All of Us* Research Program are used to predict biological age. The **lifelog age gap** is the difference between that prediction and chronological age.

A PheWAS then links the gap to genomics, proteomics, microbiomics, clinical labs, and survey data, asking which conditions track accelerated aging.
